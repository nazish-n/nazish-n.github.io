About me

Hi, my name is Nazish, and I am a final-year PhD student at MIT, advised by Fadel Adib. I develop sensing and imaging systems for diverse environments by rethinking how physical signals are captured and interpreted. I combine signal processing grounded in first principles with system-aware machine learning and practical system design for applications in industry, smart homes, and climate monitoring.

I have also had the privilege of conducting research as a summer intern at Microsoft Research and Samsung Research America. Prior to my PhD, I received an SM from MIT and a BS in Electrical Engineering from LUMS.
